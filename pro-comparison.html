Pro comparison FAQ

How matching to NBA models works.

Can I compare my form to NBA players?

Yes. Hoop Mo matches your shot to 70+ NBA players or lets you pick a model player. You get phase-by-phase comparison, similarity percentages, and visual replay side by side with pros.

How does Hoop Mo analyze my shot?

Record your shot from a side view with your phone. Hoop Mo tracks body keypoints, breaks your motion into phases like setup and release, and compares your form to NBA player models with similarity scores and coaching tips.

Is this the same as Live Shoot?

No. Live Shoot is realtime in-gym coaching with Mo. Pro Comparison focuses on matching your recorded form to NBA models. Both are in the same free app. See Live Shoot.
